Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 29, 2025

The short version: Ballpark Audio does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy describes how Ballpark Audio ("the app", "we", "our") handles information when you use the application. We are committed to your privacy and have designed the app from the ground up to operate entirely on your device with no external data collection of any kind.

Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. Ballpark Audio does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share any personal details to use the app.

Audio Files

Audio files you upload to Ballpark Audio are stored exclusively on your device in the app's private storage directory. Your audio files are never uploaded to any server, never transmitted over the internet, and never accessible to anyone other than you.

When you use the Export & Share feature, the app creates a .bpaudio package file on your device containing your audio clips. Sharing this file with others is entirely at your discretion and under your control — the app itself plays no role in that transmission.

Apple Music

Ballpark Audio can optionally access your Apple Music library to let you add songs to the Music tab. This access is requested only when you tap "Add from Apple Music" and requires your explicit permission via the iOS system prompt. The app does not browse, index, or access your music library in the background. Song references are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted externally.

Camera and Lineup Card Scanning

Ballpark Audio can optionally use your device's camera to scan a lineup card and reorder your walk-up clips automatically. The camera is requested only when you tap "Take a Photo" on the scan sheet and requires your explicit permission via the iOS system prompt. You can also pick an existing photo from your library instead.

All processing happens entirely on your device. The photo is read using Apple Vision (and Apple Foundation Models on supported devices with Apple Intelligence) to extract player names from the card. The image and the extracted text are never uploaded, stored long-term, or transmitted off your device. The app discards the photo as soon as the scan is applied.

Local Storage

Ballpark Audio stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Audio clip files — stored in the app's Application Support directory. Never leaves your device.
  • Clip metadata — names, tab assignments, team assignment, and ordering stored in a local JSON manifest. Never leaves your device.
  • Teams — names and ordering of walk-up teams you create, stored in a local JSON manifest. Never leaves your device.
  • Apple Music song references — stored in a local JSON file. Never leaves your device.
  • Played song history — stored in UserDefaults. Never leaves your device.
  • Volume boost setting — stored in UserDefaults. Never leaves your device.
  • Next-up position — stored in UserDefaults. Never leaves your device.

All of this data can be cleared at any time by deleting the app from your device (Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Ballpark Audio → Delete App).

Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or telemetry. There are no third-party SDKs, no advertising frameworks, no crash reporters, and no usage tracking of any kind embedded in Ballpark Audio.

Advertising

Ballpark Audio contains no advertising. We do not serve ads, share data with advertising networks, or use your data for any advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

Ballpark Audio does not connect to or share data with any third-party services. The only external system interactions are with on-device Apple frameworks: the Apple Music framework when you explicitly choose to add songs from your library, and the Apple Vision and Apple Foundation Models frameworks when you explicitly tap to scan a lineup card. All of these are local on-device APIs provided by iOS and do not transmit data to us or any third party.

Children's Privacy

Ballpark Audio does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever, it poses no special privacy risk to users of any age.

Data Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted externally, the security of your data is governed by your device's own security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, device encryption). We have no access to your data and therefore cannot expose it.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. Because we collect no data, changes to this policy are unlikely to affect your privacy in any way.

Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete on your behalf. All app data is on your device and fully under your control. You can delete all app data at any time by removing the app from your device.
