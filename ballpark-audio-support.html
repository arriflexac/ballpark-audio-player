Support

Everything you need to get Ballpark Audio working on game day.

Getting Started

  • Upload clips — Tap the menu button (top right) and choose Add Audio Files. Select files from your Files app or iCloud Drive. Supported formats: MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV.
  • Choose a tab — Files are added to whichever tab is active (Walk Ups, Sound FX, or Songs) when you upload.
  • Apple Music — Switch to the Music tab and tap Add from Apple Music to browse your library and add songs directly.
  • Play a clip — Tap any card to play. Tap it again to stop. Tapping a different card stops the current clip and starts the new one.
  • Your clips are saved — All audio is stored on your device. Your library persists across sessions and app restarts automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Audio won't play / nothing happens when I tap a card

Make sure your device volume is turned up and your Bluetooth speaker is connected. If you recently installed the app, allow a moment for clips to finish loading after upload.

Does audio play with the silent switch on?

Yes. Ballpark Audio uses the iOS playback audio session, which bypasses the ringer/silent switch automatically — just like the Music app or Spotify.

Does audio keep playing in the background?

Yes. Audio continues when you switch to another app, check your lineup card, or lock your screen. This is a native feature of the app — no workaround needed.

Audio sounds distorted at high volume

The Volume Boost slider amplifies audio above the normal maximum. Gain above 100% can cause clipping on clips that are already loud. Try reducing the boost slider if you hear distortion.

My walk-up order isn't saving

Clip order is saved automatically whenever you reorder. Make sure you tap Done after using reorder mode with arrow buttons. Drag-and-drop reordering in the grid saves immediately.

How do I share my soundboard with someone else?

Tap the menu button and choose Share / Export. The app packages all your clips into a single .bpaudio file. Send it via AirDrop, Messages, or Save to Files. The recipient opens the file and all clips are imported into their Ballpark Audio app.

How do I import a soundboard from someone else?

If someone sends you a .bpaudio file, tap it to open it directly in Ballpark Audio. You can also tap the menu button, choose Import Package, and select the file from your Files app.

How does the Music tab work?

The Music tab lets you add songs from your Apple Music library. Tap Add from Apple Music, browse or search, and select songs. They appear as cards just like your uploaded clips — with played tracking, fade out, and sort. Note: Apple Music requires a subscription or purchased music in your library.

What audio formats are supported?

MP3, M4A, AAC, and WAV are fully supported. Upload files from your Files app or iCloud Drive.

How do I delete a clip?

Tap the x button in the top-right corner of any card. A confirmation prompt will appear before the clip is removed.

How do I sort my clips?

In the Sound FX, Songs, and Music tabs, tap the Sort button below the grid. Choose to sort alphabetically (A-Z or Z-A) or by duration (shortest/longest first). In the Music tab you can also sort by artist.

What is the "Clear Next Up" button?

In the Walk Ups tab, the next-up highlight shows which card plays next. When the inning ends or your lineup changes, tap Clear Next Up to remove the highlight. It will reappear automatically the next time you play a Walk Up clip.

Tips

  • Fade out — Tap Fade Out in the status bar to smoothly fade the current Walk Up, Song, or Music track over 3 seconds.
  • Next up — The next Walk Up card is highlighted in blue automatically. When the last clip plays, the first clip is queued as next. Your next-up position is saved between sessions.
  • Drag and drop — In the Walk Ups tab, long-press and drag a card to reorder it directly in the grid. You can also tap Reorder Walk Ups for a list view with arrow buttons.
  • Boost slider — The boost slider (100%~400%) amplifies all audio. Use it in loud environments where maximum device volume isn't enough.
  • Reset played songs — After a game, tap Reset Played in the Songs or Music tab to clear all played markers for the next game.
  • Bluetooth — Ballpark Audio routes through the iOS playback session, so it respects your selected audio output automatically.
  • Share your setup — Use Export to create a .bpaudio package of your entire soundboard. AirDrop it to your backup operator so they have the same library ready to go.
