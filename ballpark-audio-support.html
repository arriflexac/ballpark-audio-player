Support

Everything you need to get Ballpark Audio working on game day.

Getting Started

  • Upload clips — Tap the menu button (top right) and choose Add Audio Files. Select files from your Files app or iCloud Drive. Supported formats: MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV.
  • Choose a tab — Files are added to whichever tab is active (Walk Ups, Sound FX, or Songs) when you upload.
  • Pick a team — In the Walk Ups tab, the team picker at the top lets you switch between rosters. New walk-up uploads are added to the currently selected team.
  • Scan a lineup card — In the team picker, tap Scan Lineup Card to take a photo (or pick one from your library). The app reorders today's batting order automatically and marks anyone not on the card as OUT.
  • Apple Music — Switch to the Music tab and tap Add from Apple Music to browse your library and add songs directly.
  • Play a clip — Tap any card to play. Tap it again to stop. Tapping a different card stops the current clip and starts the new one.
  • Your clips are saved — All audio is stored on your device. Your library persists across sessions and app restarts automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I manage multiple teams?

In the Walk Ups tab, tap the team picker at the top of the screen. From the dropdown you can switch teams, add a new team, rename the current team, or delete the current team. Each team has its own players, lineup order, and next-up indicator. Sound FX, Songs, and Music are shared across all teams. When you upgrade from a previous version, your existing walk-ups become a single team called "Default" — you can rename it from the picker.

How do I scan a lineup card?

Open the team picker in the Walk Ups tab and choose Scan Lineup Card. You can either take a photo with the in-app document scanner (auto-detects the card edges and corrects perspective) or pick an existing photo from your library. The app reads the card on your device — no internet required — and matches detected names to your existing walk-up clips for that team. You'll see a review screen where you can adjust any matches before tapping Apply.

What happens when I apply a scanned lineup?

Players the app matched on the card are reordered to match today's batting order at the top of your Walk Ups grid. Players on the team who are NOT on the card are automatically marked OUT (disabled) for the game — they show up in the disabled section at the bottom. To bring a disabled player back, tap the + on their card.

The scanner missed a player or matched the wrong one

On the review screen, every detected row has a small dropdown showing which clip the app picked. Tap it to override: pick a different clip from the suggestions or the full list, or choose Skip this row to ignore it. The colored dot tells you the match confidence — green is high, yellow is medium, orange is low, red is "no match". Apply only commits the matches you've confirmed.

My lineup card has fielding positions and other info — does that matter?

No. The scanner specifically ignores fielding positions (P, C, 1B, 2B, etc.), inning columns (1st, 2nd, ...), dashes, score columns, dates, opponent names, coach names, and other card scaffolding. It focuses only on the batting order and player names. If your card has more than just the lineup, that's fine.

Does the scan use the cloud or send my photos anywhere?

No. All scanning happens on your device using Apple Vision (and Apple Foundation Models on supported devices with Apple Intelligence). The photo and the extracted text never leave your phone.

What do the colors of the status bar dot mean?

The dot at the bottom of the screen reflects what's playing: grey when nothing is happening, red while a walk-up is playing, green while a song, sound effect, or Apple Music track is playing, orange while audio is fading out. A separate blue dot appears next to the "Next up" line whenever a walk-up is queued for the current team.

What is the "DRM" badge on a Music card?

Apple Music subscription tracks are protected by Apple's FairPlay DRM. These tracks play through Apple's system music player, which doesn't allow apps to fade volume programmatically. While a DRM-protected song is playing, the Fade Out button is replaced with a red Stop button so you can still halt playback quickly.

Can I fade out a DRM-protected song some other way?

Not from inside the app — Apple's restrictions prevent it. Two workarounds: (1) Pre-edit the song in GarageBand or another audio editor with a fade built in, export as MP3 or M4A, and upload it via the Songs tab — full fade support since it's your own file. (2) Buy the song from the iTunes Store (purchased downloads, not subscription) — those don't have FairPlay and play through the full audio engine with fade support.

Audio won't play / nothing happens when I tap a card

Make sure your device volume is turned up and your Bluetooth speaker is connected. If you recently installed the app, allow a moment for clips to finish loading after upload.

Does audio play with the silent switch on?

Yes. Ballpark Audio uses the iOS playback audio session, which bypasses the ringer/silent switch automatically — just like the Music app or Spotify.

Does audio keep playing in the background?

Yes. Audio continues when you switch to another app, check your lineup card, or lock your screen. This is a native feature of the app — no workaround needed.

Audio sounds distorted at high volume

The Volume Boost slider amplifies audio above the normal maximum. Gain above 100% can cause clipping on clips that are already loud. Try reducing the boost slider if you hear distortion.

My walk-up order isn't saving

Clip order is saved automatically whenever you reorder. Make sure you tap Done after using reorder mode with arrow buttons. Drag-and-drop reordering in the grid saves immediately.

How do I share my soundboard with someone else?

Tap the menu button and choose Share / Export. The app packages all your clips and teams into a single .bpaudio file. Send it via AirDrop, Messages, or Save to Files. The recipient opens the file and everything imports into their Ballpark Audio app — including the team structure for walk-ups.

How do I import a soundboard from someone else?

If someone sends you a .bpaudio file, tap it to open it directly in Ballpark Audio. You can also tap the menu button, choose Import Package, and select the file from your Files app.

How does the Music tab work?

The Music tab lets you add songs from your Apple Music library. Tap Add from Apple Music, browse or search, and select songs. They appear as cards just like your uploaded clips — with played tracking, fade out, and sort. Note: Apple Music requires a subscription or purchased music in your library.

What audio formats are supported?

MP3, M4A, AAC, and WAV are fully supported. Upload files from your Files app or iCloud Drive.

How do I disable a player for today's game?

In the Walk Ups tab, tap the x on a player's card and choose Disable. The card moves to a greyed-out "Disabled" section at the bottom of the screen, marked "OUT". That player is skipped in the walk-up sequence and next-up rotation. To bring them back, tap the + button on their disabled card — they'll be added to the end of the active lineup.

How do I delete a clip?

Tap the x button in the top-right corner of any card. For Walk Ups, you'll see options to Disable or Remove — choose Remove to permanently delete it. For Sound FX, Songs, and Music, a confirmation prompt will appear before the clip is removed.

How do I sort my clips?

In the Sound FX, Songs, and Music tabs, tap the Sort button below the grid. Choose to sort alphabetically (A-Z or Z-A) or by duration (shortest/longest first). In the Music tab you can also sort by artist.

What is the "Clear Next Up" button?

In the Walk Ups tab, the next-up highlight shows which card plays next. When the inning ends or your lineup changes, tap Clear Next Up to remove the highlight. It will reappear automatically the next time you play a Walk Up clip.

Tips

  • Use teams for multiple rosters — One app can hold walk-up sets for multiple teams. Switch between them in the team picker at the top of the Walk Ups tab. Each team keeps its own lineup, disabled players, and next-up position.
  • Scan today's lineup card — Open the team picker and tap Scan Lineup Card. The app reorders today's batters and marks anyone not on the card as OUT, all on-device. You still get a review screen before anything is applied.
  • Read the dot — The status bar dot color tells you what's happening at a glance: red = walk-up, green = song or music, orange = fading, blue (next to "Next up") = queued, grey = idle.
  • Watch the countdown — During any playback, the timer below the now-playing line counts down so you know how much is left.
  • Fade out — Tap Fade Out in the status bar to smoothly fade the current Walk Up, Song, or Music track over 3 seconds. For DRM-protected Apple Music tracks, this becomes a Stop button instead.
  • Next up — The next Walk Up card is highlighted in blue automatically. When the last clip plays, the first clip is queued as next. Your next-up position is saved per team.
  • Disable players — If a player is missing today's game, disable their Walk Up card instead of deleting it. They stay in your library and can be re-enabled with one tap for the next game.
  • Drag and drop — In the Walk Ups tab, long-press and drag a card to reorder it directly in the grid. The drop target highlights as you drag. You can also tap Reorder Walk Ups for a list view with arrow buttons.
  • Boost slider — The boost slider (100%~400%) amplifies all audio. Use it in loud environments where maximum device volume isn't enough.
  • Reset played songs — After a game, tap Reset Played in the Songs or Music tab to clear all played markers for the next game.
  • Bluetooth — Ballpark Audio routes through the iOS playback session, so it respects your selected audio output automatically.
  • Share your setup — Use Export to create a .bpaudio package of your entire soundboard. AirDrop it to your backup operator so they have the same library and team structure ready to go.
