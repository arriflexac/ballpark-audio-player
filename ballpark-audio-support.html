Support

Everything you need to get Ballpark Audio working on game day.

Getting Started

  • Upload clips — Tap the Upload button and select audio files from your Files app or iCloud Drive. Supported formats: MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV.
  • Choose a tab — Files are added to whichever tab is active (Walk Ups, Sound FX, or Songs) when you tap Upload.
  • Play a clip — Tap any card to play. Tap it again to stop. Tapping a different card stops the current clip and starts the new one.
  • Your clips are saved — All audio is stored on your device. Your library persists across sessions automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Audio won't play / nothing happens when I tap a card

Make sure your device volume is turned up. If clips appear dimmed (grey), they are still loading — wait a moment and try again. If a card shows "⚠ Unsupported", the file format is not supported in Safari. Use MP3 or M4A instead.

Audio plays on silent / ringer switch

Ballpark Audio is designed to play through the iOS "playback" audio session, which bypasses the silent/ringer switch — the same as Music or Spotify. If audio is not playing on silent, try tapping a card once to unlock the audio session, then try again.

Audio slows down or sounds distorted

This can occur if the app has been running for a long time with many clips loaded. Close and reopen the app in Safari to reset the audio session. If distortion occurs with boosted volume, reduce the Boost slider — gain above 100% can cause clipping on clips that are already at maximum loudness.

Audio stops when I switch to another app

iOS does not allow web apps to play audio in the background. Switching apps will pause playback. When you return to Ballpark Audio, tap any card to resume the audio session.

My walk-up order isn't saving

Clip order is saved automatically whenever you reorder. Make sure you tap Done after reordering to confirm your changes. If order is still not saving, check that Safari is not set to clear website data on close (Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data).

How do I share my soundboard with someone else?

Tap the Share button in the top bar. The app will encode all your clips into a single file. When the share sheet appears, choose AirDrop, Messages, or Save to Files. The recipient opens the file in Safari and all clips are ready to play — no uploading required on their end.

A clip shows "⚠ Unsupported"

The audio file could not be decoded. This usually means the file format is not supported by Safari (OGG and FLAC are not supported on iOS). Convert the file to MP3 or M4A and re-upload. You can delete the unsupported card by tapping the × button.

What audio formats are supported?

MP3, M4A, AAC, and WAV are fully supported on iOS Safari. OGG, FLAC, and WebM are not supported on iOS.

The app is slow to load

Ballpark Audio loads your clip library in two passes — cards appear immediately and become playable as each clip finishes decoding in the background. With a large library (20+ clips) the full decode may take 10–20 seconds. This is normal and only happens on first load after a refresh.

How do I delete a clip?

Tap the × button in the top-right corner of any card. A confirmation prompt will appear before the clip is removed.

Tips

  • Fade out — Tap Fade Out in the status bar to smoothly fade the current Walk Up or Song over 3 seconds.
  • Next up — The next Walk Up card is highlighted in blue automatically. When the last clip plays, the first clip is queued as next.
  • Reorder Walk Ups — Tap the Reorder button below the Walk Ups grid to enter reorder mode and use arrow buttons to rearrange your lineup.
  • Boost slider — The boost slider (100%–400%) amplifies all audio. Use it in loud environments where maximum device volume isn't enough.
  • Reset played songs — After a game, tap Reset Played in the Songs tab to clear all played markers for the next game.
  • Bluetooth — Ballpark Audio routes through the iOS playback session, so it respects your selected audio output automatically.
